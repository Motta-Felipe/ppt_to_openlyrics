Slide 1:
542   The power & the glory

This grace is mine,
this glory, earth bound heaven
sent this plan divine,
this life, this light that breaks my night.
The spirit of God,
heaven falls like a dove to my heart.

Slide 2:
This love is mine
so undeserved this glorious name.
This Son, this God, this life
this death, this vict’ry won.
For giveness has flowed and
this grace that is mine finds my heart.

Slide 3:
The power and the glory of your name.
The power and the glory of your name. 
The power and the glory of your name.
The name of the Lord, the Son of God.

Slide 4:
This life is mine
so perferct and so pure this God in me.
This glorious hope
from earth to heaven, death to life.
This future assured and secured
by this love in my heart.
