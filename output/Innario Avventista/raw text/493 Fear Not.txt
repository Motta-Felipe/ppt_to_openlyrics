Slide 1:
493   Fear not
“Fear not, for I am with you. Fear not,
for I am with you; fear not,
for I am with you”, says the Lord. (bis)

“ I have redeemed you I have called you 
by name; Chil you are mine;
When  you walk thru the waters, 
I will be there, and thru flame.
You’ll not (no way!) be drowned!
You’ll not (no way!) be burned! 
For I am with you”.
